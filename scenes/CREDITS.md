# Photo credits

The four scenes are built on photos from Wikimedia Commons, used under their Creative Commons
licences. The scenes are adaptations: cropped to 16:9, the sky extended upward or replaced with a
coded one, colour graded, and animated. Under the ShareAlike licences, the adapted scenes carry the
same licence as their photo.
